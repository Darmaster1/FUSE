How to Play FUSE 💣

Objective

Be the last player alive. Avoid holding the bomb when it explodes.

Starting the Game

  • Each player starts with 3 lives ❤️
  • One player receives the bomb with a hidden timer

If You Have the Bomb

You can:

✂️ CUT THE WIRE

  • Reduces the timer by 2 seconds
  • Increases risk of explosion

➡️ PASS THE BOMB

  • Send the bomb to another player
  • Timer is halved

Important Passing Rules

  • If timer is 2 seconds or less, next player receives it at 2 seconds
  • If timer is below 1 second, you cannot pass
  • You cannot pass to yourself or eliminated players

Explosion 💥

  • If the bomb explodes, you lose 1 life
  • At 0 lives, you are eliminated
  • Bomb resets to another player

Amplifiers ⚡ (Passive Effects)

Some players get a random amplifier each round:

🛡️
Shield
First explosion does no damage — absorbed automatically
❄️
Cold Hands
Passing reduces timer less (floor÷2 +1 instead of ÷2)
🔥
Hot Hands
Cutting the wire is slightly safer for you
⚡
Quick Reflex
Your first pass after receiving the bomb doesn't halve the timer

Winning 🏆

Last player alive wins the round.

Tips

  • Passing too late can trap others
  • Cutting is risky but sometimes necessary
  • Stay calm under pressure 😈
